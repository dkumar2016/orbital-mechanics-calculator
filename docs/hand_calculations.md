# Hand Calculations: Circular Orbits - [400 km, 20,200 Km, 35,786 Km]


Constants: mu = 398,600.4418 km^3/s^2, R_earth = 6,378.137 km
Formulas: v = sqrt(mu/r), T = 2*pi*sqrt(r^3/mu)

| Altitude (km) | r (km)     | v (km/s) | T (h / mins) |
|------------------------------------------------------|
|400            |6,778.137   |7.669     |92.56 mins    |
|20,200         |6,778.137   |3.873     |11.98 h       |
|35,786         |42,164.137  |3.075     |23.93 h       |



## Observations

- Speed decreses as altitude increases (speed is inversely proportional to altitude) and Orbital peroid increases with increase in altitude (directly proportional)
- 400 km matches the ISS orbital period of about 92 mins
